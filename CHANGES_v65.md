# Trothen v65: a warmer, printed look instead of the "AI blue"

## What was wrong
The dark blue backgrounds had a dot pattern or a grid laid over them, and the top of the front page was one big blue block. That combination (flat blue, dots, grids) is exactly the look people now recognise from AI-made websites.

## What I changed
- **The top of the front page is now light.**
  - The headline, sentence and search sit on a soft off-white, like good paper.
  - Navy is used as one solid "stage" behind the job ticket, not the whole page, so the ticket stands out and the page feels calmer.
  - A strip of yellow-and-navy hazard tape runs across the top of that stage, a small nod to building sites and trades.
- **No more dots or grids anywhere.** The navy areas now have a very fine paper grain, so the colour looks printed rather than flat on screen. The light areas do too, more faintly. This covers:
  - the navy areas: the page headers, the category list, the reviews, the closing boxes and the footer;
  - the light areas: "Popular right now", FAQ, and the content area behind About, Terms, Pricing and All Pros.
- **Page headers:** the plain yellow line under each inner page's navy header is now the same hazard tape strip.
- **Cards:** crisper edges and calmer shadows.
- **Dark mode:** the top of the front page goes back to dark ink, with the ticket's stage one shade lighter.

Nothing about how the site works has changed. My logo is unchanged.

## Tested
- On a computer and a phone, in light and dark.
- Nothing runs off the edge of the screen.
- Search still works, with no script errors.
