# Trothen v182: an animated help video for every module (customers, pros and the Admin team)

v182 goes on top of v181.

## What you asked
"Can you develop more videos, for example tokens, if users don't know how to use it, also for the customer, also for the admin. Please create a video for each module on the use of it."

## What I built

### 1. 17 new animated videos for customers and pros
They're in the same animated style as v181, with a phone version and a laptop version of each. They explain each module in plain words: what it's for, how to use it step by step, and the safety rules.

**For pros** (shown only to pros):
- **Tokens: how pros pay Trothen.** Covers:
  - what a token is (1 token = US$1);
  - why cash jobs need tokens;
  - how to buy them (in the app or with a payment code);
  - what else they can pay for;
  - what happens when you owe tokens.
- **Job matches: find work.** How a job reaches a pro, how to read it, sending a price with a note, and how the Trothen Score decides the number of matches each week.
- **Your bookings, from start to payment.** Covers:
  - confirming in time;
  - agreeing a time;
  - Leave Now (live location for that customer only);
  - asking for extra work the right way;
  - "I've finished the job" and the customer's 3 days to check;
  - tips with no commission;
  - cancelling.
- **Earnings and cash out.** Covers:
  - payout details;
  - the commission per plan (13%, 12% and 10%);
  - what "Available" means;
  - how Finance sends the money, and what happens if details are wrong;
  - the monthly top-score award.
- **Your Trothen Score and plan.** Covers:
  - what counts, out of 100;
  - matches per week by score;
  - moving up to Pro (50 jobs, rating 4.7) and Super Pro (500 jobs, rating 4.85, a year);
  - how to raise the score.
- **Your profile, prices and portfolio.** Covers:
  - the required face photo;
  - the ways to charge (per job, hour, day or trip, and up to 20 fixed prices);
  - the portfolio rules;
  - "Accepting new bookings";
  - licence reminders.
- **Drive for Trothen.** Covers:
  - getting approved;
  - going online (offline by itself after 12 hours);
  - a ride step by step with the PIN;
  - in-app and cash fares;
  - commission or a Driver Pass;
  - safety.
- **Get ready to take work.** The Get ready list, and why each item matters. It also covers guarantors (and why your own number doesn't count) and tokens for cash jobs.

**For customers** (shown to everyone):
- **Find and book a pro.** Search, ID checked, the score, reviews, saving favourites, fixed prices, and safety before booking.
- **My Bookings: from booking to done.** Covers:
  - the pro confirming;
  - agreeing a time and reminders;
  - following the pro;
  - approving and paying for extra work;
  - "Mark Complete" within 3 days, and tips;
  - rating and the trust question;
  - when you can and can't cancel.
- **Report a problem.** Talk first, and don't press Mark Complete. Then how to report with photos, the money staying held, and the three decisions (refund, release, split). It also explains why a cash job can't be refunded.
- **Protected or cash: two ways to pay.** What each means, a side-by-side comparison, and how to pay protected (Mobile Money, payment code or card).
- **Your orders, delivery and returns.** Covers:
  - the three ways to receive goods;
  - following the order;
  - checking the goods before giving the delivery code;
  - the countdown before the order completes;
  - the real return rules: within the store's return days, the seller answers within 3 days, and "changed my mind" only if the store accepts it.
- **Receipts and records.** A receipt for every payment, refund and payout, by email (or text), and where to download them again.
- **Quotes and invoices from a pro.** Quote, estimate or invoice. Accepting isn't paying. Only money paid through a Trothen booking is protected.
- **Favorite pros, reviews and points.**
- **Get help.** Covers:
  - Help videos;
  - the Trothen Assistant (a computer, not a person);
  - contacting the team;
  - Report a Problem;
  - how to spot fake "helpers".

### 3. Corrections to v181 videos
While checking every fact against the app, I found four things the v181 videos said wrong, and fixed them:
- **Business tools** said an invoice is "paid through Trothen". The app says an invoice for work outside a Trothen booking is **not** held or protected. It now says a customer accepts the quote, then books the pro, and only that booking is protected. It also now covers Inventory, the Tax report, and the invoice details.
- **Book a ride** only described paying in the app. Riders can also pay **cash**, so it now explains both.
- **Add a good to your store** said the seller always pays for a return. The real rule: faulty, wrong or not-as-described goods can always be returned within the store's return days. I also corrected the words of the real-screen video and recorded it again.
- **Sell an item** said buyers can "return" a personal item. For personal sales, a buyer who paid through Trothen reports a problem and gets their money back. It now says that.

### 4. Professional wording and pronunciation (your note on "PIN")
I spell-checked every line of all 72 animated videos, and the words on screen. No word was misspelt. I then checked that terms are written the same way everywhere (PIN, ID, Mobile Money, Trothen Score, PDF, SOS, Wi-Fi) and how the narrator says them. I fixed three things:
- **PIN.** The narrator spelled it out letter by letter ("P-I-N"). People say it as one word, "pin", so the narrator now does too. The subtitles still show "PIN".
- **Numbers with a decimal point.** A sentence like "a rating of 4.7" was cut at the decimal point, in the subtitles and in the voice ("4." … "7"). Numbers are never split now.
- **"Mobile Money"** is now always written with capitals, as on the rest of Trothen.

I made the 11 animated videos that had these words again, and recorded the payments and rides screen videos again.

### 5. A fix that protects the videos
Recording a real-screen video again used to rebuild the list of help videos from the recordings only. That would have quietly dropped every subject that has only an animated video. It now keeps them.
