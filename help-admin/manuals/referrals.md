## What this part of Admin is for

Every customer and pro has a **referral link** to invite people to Trothen. **Referrals** shows how the programme is working. Super admins only.

## The page

- **Total Referrals:** sign-ups that came through a referral link.
- **People Who've Referred Someone:** how many people have invited at least one person.
- **Top Referrers:** the people who brought the most sign-ups.
- **Recent Referral Signups:** the latest people who joined through a link, and who invited them.

## Using it

- **Thank top referrers**, and consider them for promotions.
- **Watch for abuse:** many sign-ups from one person with no activity, or the same phone numbers, can be fake accounts made for a reward. Check them in Customers & Providers and Fraud & Safety.

> **Policy:** Referral rewards (what each side gets, and when) are for Trothen to set, and must be described truthfully in any promotion.
