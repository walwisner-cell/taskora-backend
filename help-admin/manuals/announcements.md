## What this part of Admin is for

**Announcements** are official messages **from Trothen's management to the Admin team**, especially the regional managers: a new policy, a change in how to handle something, an outage. They are not seen by customers or pros (for those, use Promotions).

Every admin must read each announcement and **acknowledge** it. Management can see who has.

## Writing an announcement

1. **Title:** what it is about, for example "New cancellation policy effective Monday".
2. **Body:** what changes, from when, what the team must do, and whom to ask.
3. **Priority:**
   - **Normal:** information;
   - **High:** something the team must act on soon;
   - **Urgent:** act now (an outage, a safety issue, a fraud wave).
4. **Send at** (optional): schedule it for later. Leave blank to send now.
5. **Regions:** leave blank for everyone, or type city names separated by commas (for example Monrovia, Lagos).
6. Press `Send announcement`.

## History

Every announcement sent, with its priority, regions, and **who has read and acknowledged it**. Follow up with anyone who hasn't, especially for High and Urgent ones.

## Good practice

- One subject per announcement.
- Say **what to do**, not only what happened.
- Use Urgent only when it truly is: if everything is urgent, nothing is.
- Confidential matters (a staff issue, an investigation) are not for announcements. Speak to the people concerned directly.
