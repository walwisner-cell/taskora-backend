## What this part of Admin is for

Every pro has a **Trothen Score** from 0 to 99 (never 100). It tells customers how much they can rely on the pro, and it decides **how many new job matches** the pro gets each week. **Provider Scores** ranks every pro by score, explains each score, and lets the team **pause** a pro when needed.

> **Rule:** A low score is a reason to **call** the pro and help, not to punish them. A pause is only applied by a person.

## How the score is made

Nine parts, adding up to 100 (the total is capped at 99):

| Part | Points | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Jobs completed | 20 | Jobs finished through Trothen (full points at 50 jobs). |
| Customer trust rating | 20 | Customers who answered "yes" to "Do you trust this pro?" after a job. |
| Low cancellation rate | 15 | Jobs the pro cancelled. A **protected** cancellation reason (a real emergency) doesn't count against them. |
| Arrived on time | 15 | Jobs where the pro pressed **Arrived**. |
| Identity verification | 10 | Their ID is approved. |
| Responding to jobs | 7 | How often and how fast they answer job matches. |
| Repeat customers | 5 | Customers who booked them again. |
| License / documents | 4 | A valid licence, for licensed trades. Full points for other trades. |
| Background check | 4 | Guarantors confirmed by the team (Verification Queue). |

**A new pro is not punished for having no history.** Until they have done enough jobs, the parts based on jobs count as full and move towards their real results as jobs come in. A pro with fewer completed jobs than the **New-pro rule** shows **"New on Trothen"** instead of a score.

Scores are updated **every day**. `Recompute now` updates them all at once.

## New job matches each week

| Score | New matches a week |
|---|---|
| 90 to 99 | Unlimited |
| 85 to 89 | 30 |
| 80 to 84 | 20 |
| 75 to 79 | 15 |
| 70 to 74 | 10 |
| 65 to 69 | 7 |
| 60 to 64 | 5 |
| 50 to 59 | 4 |
| 40 to 49 | 3 |
| 30 to 39 | 2 |
| 20 to 29 | 1 |
| Below 20 | **None: new matches are paused automatically** |
| New on Trothen | 10 |

This is automatic. It only limits **new** matches: the pro keeps their bookings in progress.

## The list

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Rank | Position by score. |
| Provider, City, Category | The pro. |
| Score | The score, colour-coded; or **New** with a provisional score. |
| Status | Good standing; **Getting fewer new matches each week** (41 to 60); **Few new matches (automatic)** (20 to 40); **New matches paused (automatic)** (below 20); or **On hold**. |
| Action | `Breakdown` and `Place on Hold`. |

A search box filters the list.

### Breakdown

Shows the nine parts for one pro: what they scored on each, and why. Use it before calling a pro: it tells you **what** to talk about (for example "you cancelled 4 of your last 10 jobs", or "you never press Arrived").

### Calling a pro with a low score

From **40 or below**, the status asks you to **call**:

1. Open the Breakdown. Find the parts that are low.
2. Call the pro. Explain what pulls their score down, kindly and clearly.
3. Ask what happened: illness, a broken tool, misunderstanding the app.
4. Explain what will raise it: press Arrived, don't cancel, answer matches, ask customers to rate.
5. Note the call for your team.

### Place on Hold

A hold pauses **all** new bookings and payouts (not only matches). Use it for a real risk (safety, fraud), not for a low score alone. Release it in Customers & Providers.

## New-pro rule

**Jobs needed, completed on Trothen** (5 by default): until a pro has completed this many, they show "New on Trothen". Changing it does not update scores at once: press `Recompute now` after saving, or wait for the daily update.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Placing a hold because a score is low. The weekly limits already handle it.
- Calling a pro without opening the Breakdown first.
- Telling a pro their score is "bad" without saying what to change.
