## What this part of Admin is for

**Settings** has two parts:

- **your own admin account** (every admin): your profile, password, two-factor sign-in, and sessions;
- **the platform** (super admins, and some parts for customer service): booking windows, support contacts, homepage wording and photos, the footer, idle sign-out, the Terms, the Privacy Policy, the handbook, and data backups.

> **Rule:** Platform settings change what **every** customer and pro sees. Change them only when you are sure, and tell the team.

## Your account

- **Profile:** your full name and email. `Save Changes`.
- **Two-Factor Authentication:** a 6-digit code is required every time you sign in, besides your password. It **can't be turned off** for an admin account.
- **Change Password:** your current password, then the new one twice. Use a long password you use nowhere else.
- **Sessions:** `Sign out of all devices` signs you out everywhere, including any device you don't recognise. Use it if you think someone else used your account.

## Platform settings (super admin)

### Provider Booking Confirmation Window

How long a pro has to **accept or decline** a new booking before it is cancelled automatically and the customer refunded. It depends on how soon the job is: **within 24 hours** (1 hour by default), **within 2 to 7 days** (4 hours), **beyond 7 days** (24 hours). The deadline never goes past the job's start. A category can have its own window.

### Support Chat Contact Info

The **WhatsApp number** (digits only, with the country code), the **phone number shown to visitors**, and the **support email**, used by "Chat with us" and "Call". The page warns while they are still **placeholder numbers**: replace them before launch. You can set different contacts for **Dispute**, **Onboarding** and **Promotion** questions, and for a **specific city**.

### Homepage content and photos

The homepage wording (headline, rotating words, mission, and every section), and its photos: the hero background, the mission photo, and a photo for each popular category. Changes are **published at once**: check spelling, and that every claim is true.

### Site Footer

The company name, location, support email and year, shown at the bottom of every page and in PDFs and agreements. A preview shows the result.

### Signing out when idle

How long a session stays open with no activity: **admins** 2 to 30 minutes (5 recommended), **customers and pros** 5 to 120 minutes (15 recommended). One minute before, a "Still there?" warning appears. Keep the recommended values.

### Site Content: About Us and Terms of Service

The **About Us** page, and the **Terms of Service** for customers and for providers (separate documents: each sees only their own). A line starting with `#` becomes a heading; `**text**` becomes bold. **Have a lawyer review the Terms before relying on them.** When the Terms change, everyone is asked to accept the new version.

### Privacy Policy

Shown from the footer, the account menu, the agreement screen and at /privacy. Super admins and customer service can edit it. It fills in the support email and the ID-file period automatically. **Have it reviewed by a lawyer before launch.**

### Platform Handbook

The getting-started guide (a PDF) offered to every new customer and pro. `Upload PDF` replaces it everywhere.

### Data backups

A copy of the records is taken **every night**; the newest **14 days** are kept. They sit on the same disk, so they protect against mistakes, not against losing the disk. Uploaded photos and ID files are not included.

- `Take a copy now` before any big change.
- `Download` a copy **every week**, and keep it somewhere else (another computer, a USB drive, or a cloud folder Trothen controls). Treat the download as confidential: it holds personal data.

### Maintenance Tools

`Fix Old Notifications` adds the missing Admin link to older notifications. Safe at any time.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Leaving placeholder support numbers live.
- Editing the Terms or the Privacy Policy without a lawyer.
- Never downloading a backup.
- Raising the admin idle time to "save time".
