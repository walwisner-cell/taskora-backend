## What this part of Admin is for

A **promotion** is a message shown inside Trothen, on the Overview page of customers, pros, or both, for example "Refer a friend this month" or a new service. It can also be **sent by email**.

> **Rule:** Everything in a promotion must be **true**, clear and fair. A promotion that promises what Trothen won't do is false advertising.

## Who can post, and where it shows

- A **super admin** can post **platform-wide**, or to one city (or country) by typing it in **Region**.
- A **regional admin**'s promotion always goes to **their own area**: their city, or their whole country for a country-wide admin.

## Posting a promotion

1. Press `+ New Promotion`.
2. **Title:** short and clear, for example "Refer a friend this month".
3. **Message:** what the offer is, who it is for, and **until when**. Say the conditions in plain words.
4. **Image** (optional): use the upload button. Only images Trothen may use.
5. **Show to:** Customers & Providers, Customers only, or Providers only.
6. **Region** (super admin): leave blank for everyone, or type a city to target it.
7. **End date** (optional): it stops showing after this date. It must be in the future.
8. **Also send this by email:** tick to email it to everyone who sees it.
9. Post it.

### About the email

- It goes to every **customer and pro** in the audience and region who has an email address.
- People who switched **Promotions** off in their notification settings **are not emailed**. That is their choice, and marketing-consent rules require it.
- Use email sparingly: too many emails and people stop reading them.

> **Policy:** Changes to the **Terms** or the **Privacy Policy** are not promotions. Those are made in Settings, and everyone is asked to accept the new version. A promotion is for offers and news.

## Managing promotions

The list shows each promotion, who it is for, its region, end date, and how many emails were sent. `Deactivate` stops showing one at once; `Activate` shows it again.

## Mistakes to avoid

- A promotion with no end date for an offer that ends.
- Conditions hidden or missing.
- An image Trothen has no right to use.
- Emailing everyone for small news.
