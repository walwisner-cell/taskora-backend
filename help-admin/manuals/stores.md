## What this part of Admin is for

Pros can open a **store** in Trothen and sell **goods** (phones, clothes, building materials and so on). Pros can also offer **extra skills** besides their main kind of work. Before customers see a store, a good, or a licensed extra skill, **a person in the team checks it here**.

Market listings by ordinary people (second-hand goods) are checked in **Admin → Market**, not here.

> **Rule:** Approve only what is **genuine, legal to sell, and shown in the photos**. When you turn something down, give a reason the pro can act on.

## The page, from top to bottom

1. **Store returns:** returns the seller and buyer couldn't settle.
2. **Market listings, sellers and orders:** a shortcut to Admin → Market.
3. **Stores waiting for approval.**
4. **Goods waiting to be checked.**
5. **Extra skills waiting to be checked.**
6. **Stores:** every store.
7. **What stores have sold.**
8. **Recent decisions.**
9. **Store purchase fee**, banned words and store rules (settings).
10. **Trothen's share of delivery.**

## Stores waiting for approval

A new store is **hidden until approved**. Approve a store if its **name and products are genuine and lawful**. Turn it down, with a reason, if:

- the name copies a well-known brand the pro doesn't represent;
- the name or description has phone numbers, links, or ways to pay outside Trothen;
- it sells what is never allowed (below).

## Goods waiting to be checked

Each good shows its **photos**, **name**, **description**, **details** (model, storage, size, material…), **price** and **stock**. Nobody can buy it until it is approved.

Check:

1. **The photos show the real good**, not a picture taken from the internet or a catalogue. Click a photo to see it bigger.
2. **The details make sense** for this good and this price. A price far below normal can mean stolen or fake goods.
3. **The name and description** have no banned goods, no phone numbers, and no links taking buyers outside Trothen.
4. **It is legal to sell.** Never allowed: **weapons, drugs, medicines, alcohol, tobacco, counterfeit goods, protected wildlife**.

Then `Approve`, or `Turn down` with a reason the seller sees, for example: "The photos are from a website. Please add your own photos of the phone you are selling."

## Extra skills waiting to be checked

These are **licensed trades** (for example electrical work or gas fitting). Approve **only if you have seen a licence that covers this trade**. A licence for one trade does not cover another. If no licence is attached, turn it down and say what is needed.

## Store returns

Buyers can return goods within the store's **return days**. Faulty, wrong or not-as-described goods can always be returned. When the seller and the buyer don't agree, the return is **escalated** and waits here, marked **to decide**.

Each line shows the order number, the store and seller, the buyer, the goods, the reason, the amount and their notes. Decide:

| Button | Result |
|---|---|
| `Refund` | The buyer is refunded for the returned goods. |
| `Refuse` | The return is refused, and the seller is paid. |

Each needs a **note both sides see**. Decide on the evidence: the reason given, the photos, and what the listing promised.

### The minimum return window (super admin)

**Every store takes back faulty goods for at least (days)**: 7 by default. A seller's money for an order is paid **after** this window, so a refund is always possible. 0 means no returns after an order completes.

## Stores, and what they have sold

- **Stores:** each store, its owner, and whether it is open.
- **What stores have sold:** confirmed bookings with goods, the value of goods sold, and the purchase fees paid by customers and by pros.
- **Recent decisions:** the latest approvals and turn-downs, and who made them.

## Settings (super admin)

- **Store purchase fee:** Trothen takes **one flat fee** for each booking that includes store goods (US$5 by default). **No commission is taken on goods.** Choose **who pays** it: the customer (added at checkout) or the pro (taken at payout).
- **Stores switched on:** switching stores off hides every store and stops goods being added to new bookings. It does not change bookings already made.
- **Extra banned words:** a good or store with one of these words is refused. Always refused, whatever is written: weapons, drugs, medicines, alcohol, tobacco, counterfeit goods, protected wildlife.
- **Store rules a pro must agree to:** one rule per line. When they change, every pro is asked to agree again. Have a lawyer review them before launch.
- **Trothen's share of delivery:** when a seller charges for delivery, or a customer picks a driver, the customer pays Trothen this percentage of the delivery price (9% by default). Nothing is taken on the goods.

Changing the fee, or who pays it, applies to **new** bookings only.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Approving a good with internet photos.
- Approving a licensed skill without seeing the licence.
- A turn-down reason with nothing to act on ("Not allowed").
- Deciding a return without reading both sides.
