## What this part of Admin is for

The **Market** is where people sell their own things (second-hand goods), stores sell goods, and hosts offer **places to stay** (Rentals). **Admin → Market** is where the team keeps it safe:

- check listings the safety checks **held back**, and listings people **reported**;
- approve **places to stay** before guests can book them;
- **stop sellers** who keep breaking the rules;
- follow **orders** with a problem, and **returns**;
- handle reports of **stolen items**.

> **Rule:** Take down what breaks the rules, and **always say why**: the seller sees your reason and can fix it. Stop a seller only when they keep breaking the rules. Every action is recorded under **Team activity**.

## At a glance

**Needs the team:** listings held or reported, store goods waiting, orders with a problem, returns for the team, and stolen-item reports open. Click a number to go straight there.

**The Market now:** listings and store goods on sale, stores open, orders in progress and completed, the value sold in 30 days, listings taken down and sellers stopped.

## To check: held and reported listings

A new personal listing is **held for the team** (not shown to buyers) when the safety checks find a sign of a scam:

- priced **far below** (under a quarter of) what the same kind of item usually sells for there;
- a **photo another seller already uploaded** (stolen photos);
- an item of **$500 or more** from a seller with **no completed sales**;
- **too many new listings** from a brand-new account;
- words used in scams: paying outside Trothen, a money transfer service, gift cards, an advance fee, an overpayment, a fake delivery fee, or asking for a code.

Each listing shows **why it was held**, then the description, the details and the photos.

1. Read why it was held.
2. Look at every photo. Are they real photos of this item, not from a shop's website?
3. Read the description and details. Do they make sense together, and with the price?
4. Look at the seller: how long on Trothen, any earlier reports, ID checked or not.

Then:

- `Approve` (or `Show it again`): it appears in the Market.
- `Take it down`: write why. "Why? The seller sees this, so they can fix it."
- `Stop this seller`: if this seller keeps breaking the rules (see Sellers).

> **Example:** a reason for taking down: "The photos are from an online shop. Please add your own photos of the item you are selling."

## Rentals: places to stay

A host's place is shown to guests only after the team approves it. Each place shows its photos, the **private address** and **pin on the map**, the price, the house rules, and whether the host made the **ownership declaration** (that they own it or may rent it out).

Check:

1. The **photos** are of this place, and match the description (rooms, bathrooms, water, power).
2. The **pin** is where the address says.
3. The host **declared** they may rent it out. If not, don't approve.
4. The host's **ID** is checked.

Then:

- `Approve: show it to guests`.
- `Turn down`: "Why is this place turned down? The host reads this and can fix it." For example: "The photos are of a different house."
- `Take down` (a live place): "Why is this place taken down? The host reads this."

## Sellers

Sellers with the **most reports and problems come first**.

- `Stop selling` / `Stop this seller`: choose **for how long** (7 days, 30 days, 90 days, or until the team lifts it) and say **why** (the seller sees it). Stopping a seller **takes their listings off sale and closes their store**. **Orders already paid for carry on.**
- `Allow selling again`: lifts the stop.

> **Policy:** How many warnings before a seller is stopped, and for how long, is for Trothen to set. Until it is set: take down and explain the first time; stop for 7 days the second time; 30 days or longer for anything dangerous or a scam.

## Orders and returns

- Orders with a **problem** say "Problem reported. Decide it in Disputes". Press `Open Disputes`: the money waits for a decision there.
- A **return** the buyer and seller couldn't settle has `Decide the return`: refund the buyer, or refuse the return so the seller is paid, with a note both sides see.

## Stolen items

Anyone can report an item as **stolen**, with details. Each report shows what was found. Look for listings or goods that match it (search in **Listings** and **Store goods**), take down any that match, and keep the details for the police if the owner asks.

Close it with `Close the report`, saying **what was found or done**: "The person who reported it sees this."

## Team activity

Every take-down, approval, stop and decision, with who did it, when and why. Use it to check the work, and to answer a seller who asks why something happened.

## Fees

Trothen's fees on Market sales. Changes apply to new sales only. Super admins set them.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Taking something down without a reason the seller can act on.
- Approving a place without checking the pin and the declaration.
- Stopping a seller for a first, small mistake.
- Deciding an order problem here instead of in Disputes.
