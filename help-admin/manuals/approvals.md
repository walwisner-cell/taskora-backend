## What this part of Admin is for

When someone creates an account, they appear in **User Approvals**. You look at each new sign-up and decide whether the account should be allowed on Trothen.

> **Rule:** Approving an account gives **access only**. It does **not** check who the person is. The person becomes **Verified** only when their **ID** is approved in the **Verification Queue**. The page says so at the top.

So there are two different checks:

| Check | Where | What it decides |
|---|---|---|
| Account approval | User Approvals | Can this account use Trothen at all? (not obviously false, not a duplicate, not banned before) |
| ID check | Verification Queue | Is this person who they say they are? (Verified, "ID checked") |

## Who can approve

- The **Verification team** and the **Customer service team**.
- The **regional admin** of the person's city or country. The top of the page shows the **Region** you cover ("All Locations" for a super admin).
- A **super admin**.
- A **custom admin** with "User Approvals" in their permissions.

A person outside your area is refused: "That user is outside the area you manage".

## The list

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| User | The name on the account. |
| Role | Customer or Provider (pro). |
| City, Country | Where they signed up. |
| Submitted | When they signed up. |
| Account | Pending, Approved or Rejected. |
| ID | Where their ID stands: **No ID uploaded yet**, **waiting** in the Verification Queue, or **approved**. |
| Action | `Approve` and `Reject`. |

Work **oldest first**.

## What to check before approving

1. **The name looks real:** a first name and a family name, not "test", "aaa", random letters, or a business name in place of a person's name.
2. **The phone number fits the country:** for Liberia, for example, a Liberian number.
3. **The city is in the country** they chose.
4. **It is not a second account** of someone already on Trothen: search the name, phone and email in **Customers & Providers**. Look for someone who was **rejected or suspended** before and is signing up again.
5. **For a pro:** the kind of work they chose makes sense.

> **Tip:** When something looks unusual but not false (a spelling mistake in the city, a nickname), approve, and let the ID check settle who they are.

## Approve

Press `Approve`.

- The account can use Trothen.
- If the person **has no approved ID yet**, they stay **Unverified**, and a window tells you so:
  - "their ID is waiting in the Verification Queue" (then you are taken there to check it); or
  - "they have not uploaded an ID yet. They have been told to do it in their Verification section."
- The person is told:
  - a customer: to verify their identity before they can post a job or book;
  - a pro: to verify their identity, so customers see "ID checked" and they appear in search; and, if they have no profile photo, to add one.

## Reject

Press `Reject` and write the reason. **Rejecting blocks the person from signing in.** They are told: "Your account application was not approved:" followed by your reason.

Reject only when the account is clearly false, a duplicate, or someone who must not be on Trothen. Write a reason that is true and neutral:

| Situation | A good reason |
|---|---|
| A second account | "You already have a Trothen account. Please sign in with it, or contact support for help." |
| Clearly false details | "The details on this account could not be accepted. Contact support if you think this is a mistake." |
| Banned before | "This account can't be approved. Contact support for more information." |

Never write an accusation ("you are a scammer"). If you suspect fraud, reject with a neutral reason, and report it in **Fraud & Safety**.

> **Policy:** Who may be refused an account, and for which reasons, is for Trothen to set. Until it is set, refuse only false, duplicate or previously banned accounts, and approve everyone else so their ID can be checked.

## After approval: the ID

Most people send their ID right after signing up. When the ID column says it is **waiting**, go to the **Verification Queue** and check it there (see the Verification manual).

## Mistakes to avoid

- Thinking an approved account is a verified person. It isn't.
- Rejecting because a name is unusual or foreign. Names differ; the ID check settles it.
- Rejecting without searching for an earlier account first.
- A reason that gives the person nothing to act on.

## Quick checklist

1. Oldest first.
2. Real-looking name, phone fits the country, city in the country.
3. Search Customers & Providers for an earlier account.
4. Fine: Approve. Then check their ID in the Verification Queue when it arrives.
5. False, duplicate or banned: Reject, with a neutral reason.
