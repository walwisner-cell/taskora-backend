## What this part of Admin is for

Ads on Trothen's home page, marked **"Sponsored"**, come from two places, and both arrive here:

- **Businesses** that use the **"Advertise here"** form on the home page;
- **Pros** who place an ad for their own work from the app. These need a **content check** before they go live ("From a pro · needs a content check").

The team checks every ad, **approves** it (it goes live on the home page), or **turns it down** with a reason, and **takes down** a live ad when needed.

> **Rule:** An ad must be **true**, **lawful** and **fair**. Trothen doesn't advertise what it doesn't allow in the Market (weapons, drugs, medicines, alcohol, tobacco, counterfeit goods), nor gambling, nor anything misleading.

## The list

Each ad or request shows:

- the **headline** (or the business name), the contact person, and when it came in;
- the **message** or what they want to advertise;
- the **city** it is for (or every visitor);
- its **status**: New, Contacted, **Live** (with the days left, how many times it was **seen** and **pressed**), or **Closed** (turned down, or ended).

`Details` opens the full request.

## Checking an ad

1. Read it fully: who is advertising, what, where, and the wording.
2. **Is it genuine?** A real business or pro, a real product or service.
3. **Is it lawful and allowed?** (See the rule above.)
4. **Is it true?** No promises that can't be checked ("best in Liberia", "100% guaranteed"), no prices that are wrong.
5. **Is the link safe?** It must go to the advertiser's own page, not somewhere asking for payment details.

## Approve: putting an ad live

Press `Approve`. A window opens, **Approve and Go Live**:

- **Price** and **Currency**: what the advertiser pays (for a business request);
- **Headline** shown on the homepage, and the **line under the headline**;
- **Link** (optional).

It starts showing at once: to visitors in the chosen **city** first, or to **every visitor**. It is marked "Sponsored".

> **Note:** While payments are in test mode, no real money is taken for an ad.

## Turn down

Press `Turn down`, and say **why** (3 to 300 characters). **If a pro placed it, they see your reason**, and can change the ad and send it again. For example: "The ad says 'guaranteed lowest price', which we can't check. Please remove it and send the ad again."

## Take down a live ad

`Take down` stops a live ad at once, for example after a complaint, or if the advertiser asks.

## Business requests

For a request from a business:

1. Reply within **1 working day** by email or phone, with Trothen's advertising options.
2. Press `Mark contacted` (or `Mark Contacted`).
3. When it is agreed, `Approve` it with the agreed price. If not, `Close` it. `Reopen` brings it back.

> **Policy:** Advertising prices, sizes, how long ads run, and what may be advertised are for Trothen to set, and the terms need a lawyer's review. Don't promise prices or dates that haven't been agreed.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Approving an ad without reading every word and checking the link.
- A turn-down reason the pro can't act on.
- Leaving a business request without a reply.
