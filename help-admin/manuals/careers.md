## What this part of Admin is for

People who want to **work for Trothen** apply on the **Careers** page. Their applications arrive here. The **HR team** (and super admins) handle them.

> **Rule:** Applications hold personal information: names, contact details, work history. Only the people hiring see them, and they are used only for that job.

## The page

- **Total Applications** and **New**.
- **All Applications**: each with the person's name, contact details, the job, their message or CV details, when it came in, and its **status**.

Set the **status** with the list on each row:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| New | Not looked at yet. |
| Reviewed | Read, and being considered. |
| Contacted | Someone has been in touch with the applicant (for example, invited to an interview). |
| Rejected | Not chosen. |

## Working an application

1. Read it fully.
2. Decide with the hiring manager whether to invite them to an interview.
3. Set it to **Reviewed** once you have read it.
4. Contact them, and set it to **Contacted**.
5. When it is decided, tell the applicant the outcome (always, even when it is no). Set it to **Rejected** if they were not chosen.

> **Policy:** Trothen's hiring rules, interview steps and how long applications are kept are for Trothen to set, in line with employment and data protection law. Until set, tell every applicant the outcome within 3 weeks, and don't keep applications after the job is filled unless the applicant agreed.

## Fair hiring

- Judge only on the skills and experience the job needs.
- Never ask about or decide on religion, ethnicity, tribe, pregnancy, disability, marital status or politics.
- Keep notes about candidates factual and polite: the candidate may ask to see them.
