## What this part of Admin is for

Businesses and institutions (a hotel group, an NGO, a property company) can ask for a **Custom plan** with the **Contact Sales** button. Their requests arrive in **Sales Inquiries**. When a deal is agreed, the inquiry becomes an **Organization** account. The **Sales team** and super admins handle both.

## Sales Inquiries

At the top: **Total Inquiries**, **New**, **Contacted**, and **Converted to Org**. Filter **All Inquiries** by status: New, Contacted or Closed.

Each inquiry shows the organization, the contact person and their details, how many people or seats, their needs, and the status.

### Working an inquiry

1. Reply within **1 working day**.
2. Understand what they need: how many staff or members, which services, which cities.
3. Agree the **price**, the **commission rate** and the terms with your manager.
4. Press `Mark Contacted` when you have replied, and `Close` it if it doesn't go ahead (`Reopen` if they come back).
5. Record what was agreed in `Deal Notes`: the price, the currency, the commission rate and anything else agreed.
6. Once an agreed price is recorded, `Convert to Organization` appears. Convert it only when the contract is signed. Afterwards, `View Organization` opens it.

> **Policy:** Custom prices, commission rates and contract terms are for Trothen to approve, and the contract needs a lawyer's review. Never promise a price or rate you haven't had approved.

## Organizations

At the top: the number of **Organizations**, the **Total Seats**, the **Combined MRR** (monthly recurring revenue of active organizations), and the **Avg. Commission Rate**.

Each organization shows its status (**Active** or **Suspended**), its **account manager**, the **agreed price** and currency, its seats, and its commission rate. Its members get the organization's commission rate in place of their plan's.

- Keep the account manager up to date: they are the organization's contact at Trothen.
- **Suspend** an organization that stopped paying or ended its contract; its members return to their own plans.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Promising a custom rate before it is approved.
- Converting an inquiry before the contract is signed.
- Leaving an inquiry without a reply.
