## Welcome to Admin

Admin is where the Trothen team keeps the platform safe, fair and working: checking new people and their IDs, deciding disputes, sending pros their money, and running the business. This manual covers what every team member needs on their **first day**, and the **Overview** page you see when you sign in.

> **Rule:** Everything you see in Admin is confidential. Customers' and pros' details, money, IDs and disputes stay inside Trothen. Never photograph the screen, copy it into another app, or talk about a person outside your work.

## Your account

- You sign in with your own email and password. **Never share your account.** Everything you do is recorded under your name.
- Admin accounts are **signed out after 5 minutes** with no activity, to protect the data if you leave your computer.
- Your account shows only the parts of Admin your role needs. If you need a part you can't see, ask a super admin to add it to your permissions.
- Some accounts cover **one city or one country**: you then see only the people and the activity of that area.

## Finding your way

- The **menu on the left** lists the parts of Admin you can open.
- Under the heading of each page are two buttons:
  - `▶ Watch how it works`: a video on that page;
  - `📘 Manual`: the manual for that page, like this one.
- **Help videos** (at the bottom of the menu) lists every video and manual you can open.
- Long lists have a **search box** above them: type a name, a number or a word.

## The Overview page

The first page after sign-in. The title says whose area it covers: **Global Overview: All Locations** for a super admin, or your city or country.

### The four numbers

| Number | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Total Users | Customers and pros in your area. | Watch the trend. |
| Pending Approvals | New sign-ups waiting to be approved. | Open User Approvals. |
| Open Disputes | Problems waiting for a decision. Money is held while they wait. | Open Disputes. |
| Total Booked Value | The value of bookings that went ahead. While payments are in test mode, it says "simulated, payments not live". | — |

### Locations (super admins)

Each city where Trothen works: its country, its regional **admin**, what they **cover** (one city, or the whole country), the number of users, and whether it is **Active**. A super admin can `Make country-wide`, `Suspend` or `Delete` a location. Locations and their admins are explained in the Locations & Admins manual.

### Pending Approvals and Open Disputes

The latest of each, so you can start work straight from the Overview with `Approve`, `Reject` or `Resolve`. They work exactly as in User Approvals and Disputes.

## Your notifications

The bell at the top shows what needs you: a new ID to check, a dispute, a payout waiting, a high fraud flag. Urgent ones say so ("Urgent, review within 2 hours").

## Announcements

When the company publishes an announcement for the team, you must read it and press to acknowledge it. Super admins can see who has read it.

## Every day

1. Open the Overview: what is waiting?
2. Do the work of your role, **oldest first**: IDs, approvals, disputes, payouts.
3. Check your notifications for anything urgent.
4. Before you leave, make sure nothing urgent is left half-done, and sign out.

## Asking for help

- Read the **Manual** for the page.
- Watch the **video**.
- Ask your manager, or a super admin.
- If something in Admin looks broken, a super admin can run **System Check**.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Sharing your sign-in, or leaving Admin open on a shared computer.
- Doing a task your role doesn't cover because "the button was there". Ask first.
- Deciding about someone you know personally. Hand it to a colleague.
