## What this part of Admin is for

**Reports** shows how Trothen is doing: what customers book, where demand isn't being met, which categories and pros perform best, and whether the platform is healthy. Use it to decide where to find new pros, which categories to promote, and what to report to management.

> **Note:** While payments are in **test mode**, money figures are simulated. Treat them as practice numbers until real payments are live.

## Report Builder

Build a report from booking data:

- **From date** and **To date**;
- **Category** (for example Plumbing);
- **City**;
- **Status**: any, completed, active, cancelled or pending confirmation;
- **Provider Tier**: any, Starter, Pro or Super Pro.

Press `Run Report`. The result shows the bookings that match, with the totals.

## Customers Waiting With No Pro

Jobs posted in the **last 30 days** that **had no pro to send them to**, by category and place. This is where Trothen is **losing customers**: recruit pros in those categories and places first.

## Demand by Category

The categories with the most bookings, as bars. **Select a bar** to filter the tables below to that category.

## Jobs Booked, Last 30 Days

The bookings of the last month, and their total.

## Category Performance

For each category with bookings: jobs booked, **GMV** (the value of bookings that went ahead), the average job value, the number of pros, and their average rating. It also says how many categories have **no bookings yet**.

## Top Providers

Pros ranked by **completed jobs**, with jobs booked, GMV and rating. A search box filters the list. Use it to thank top pros, and for the monthly top-score award.

## Platform Health

Live checks of the service: response time and status. It is a quick check, not a history: **System Check** goes deeper.

## Good practice

- Compare the same period each month (for example the 1st to the last day).
- Explain a number before sharing it: what it counts and what it doesn't.
- Reports with people's names stay inside Trothen.
