## What this part of Admin is for

**Customers & Providers** is the directory of everyone on Trothen. It is where you **look someone up** before deciding anything about them, and where you **act on an account**: reveal their contact details, pause or suspend them, reset their password, set a custom commission rate for a pro, or invite a customer to VIP.

> **Rule:** Everything here is personal information. Look only at the people your work needs, and only for that work. Never share one person's details with another.

## Who can do what

| Action | Who |
|---|---|
| See the directory | Admins whose role covers people: regional admins, super admins, the teams whose menu shows it, and custom admins with "Customers & Providers". |
| `🔓 Reveal Contact Info` | All of the above **except** the Customer service, Sales and HR teams. Every reveal is in the **Access Logs**. |
| `⏸️ Place on Hold` / `▶️ Release Hold` | Admins who can manage the person. |
| `Suspend Account` / `Reactivate Account` | Admins who can manage the person. |
| `Reset password` | **Super admins only.** |
| `💲 Propose Custom Commission Rate` | Regional admins and super admins. **Only a super admin can approve it.** |
| `Grant VIP` / `Revoke VIP` | **Super admins only.** |

A team member who covers one city or one country sees and acts only on people in that area.

## The directory

At the top: the number of people, and buttons to show **All roles**, **Customers** or **Providers**, and `Clear`. A search box filters the list as you type (a name, part of an email, a city).

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Name | The name on the account. |
| Contact | The email, **masked** (for example j\*\*\*\*\*@example.com). |
| Role | Customer or Provider. |
| Category, Tier | For a pro: the kind of work, and the plan (Starter, Pro, Super Pro). |
| City, Country | Where they are. |
| Joined | When they signed up. |
| Verified | **Verified, ID reviewed**, or not verified. |
| Status | Active, On hold, or Suspended. |
| Action | `View`, `Reset password` (super admin), `Suspend`. |

## A person's details

Press `View`. The window shows:

- **Email** and **Phone**, masked until revealed; **Address**;
- **City / Country**, **Joined**, **Terms Accepted** (the version and date);
- **Identity Verified** and **Phone Verified**;
- for a pro: **Category**, **Specialties**, **Hourly Rate**, **Rating**, **Tier**, **License Expiry**, **Insurance Expiry**, and any **Custom Commission Rate**;
- **Account Status**: Active, On hold (with the reason and since when), or Suspended.

### Reveal Contact Info

Press `🔓 Reveal Contact Info` only when you **need** to contact the person: to check a payout, follow up a dispute, or confirm who they are. The full email, phone and address appear, and **your name, the person and the time are written in the Access Logs**.

## Acting on an account

### Place on Hold, and Release Hold

A **hold** pauses what someone can **start**: new bookings and payouts. They can still sign in, see their history and contact support.

- Holds are placed **automatically** by a High fraud flag (see Fraud & Safety), or by you with `⏸️ Place on Hold` and a reason. The person sees the reason.
- `▶️ Release Hold` lifts it. The person is told they can book and accept jobs again.

Use a hold for a **present risk you are still looking into**. A hold that is forgotten hurts an honest person: release it as soon as you have decided.

### Suspend Account, and Reactivate Account

**Suspending** stops the account from being used at all. It is a serious step.

- If the person has **bookings in progress**, every super admin is told how many, because each one needs a decision (refund, another pro, and so on).
- `Reactivate Account` restores it.

> **Policy:** When to suspend, and who may decide, is for Trothen to set. Until it is set, suspend only with a super admin's agreement, and write the reason in your team's notes.

### Reset password (super admin)

For someone who can't sign in and can't reset it by email:

1. **Confirm it is really them** first, for example by calling the phone number on their account and asking details only they would know.
2. Press `Reset password` and confirm.
3. A **one-time password** is shown **once**. Give it to them **directly**, by phone or in person. Never in a message others can read.
4. They are signed out everywhere and must choose a new password at their next sign-in.

> **Warning:** A password reset for the wrong person hands them someone else's account and money. Never reset because someone asks in a chat or by email alone.

### Custom commission rate (pros)

A pro can have a commission rate different from their plan, for example a pro bringing many customers.

1. A regional admin or super admin presses `💲 Propose Custom Commission Rate`, types the rate (between 0% and 50%), and explains why (at least 10 characters).
2. **Every super admin is told**, and one of them approves or rejects it (`✅ Approve Custom Rate`, or reject). The person who proposed it is told the result.
3. Only one proposal can wait at a time for each pro.

Nobody can give a pro a custom rate alone: it always takes a proposal and a super admin's approval.

### Super Pro approval

When a pro becomes eligible for **Super Pro**, the window shows `🏆 Approve Super-Pro (eligible since …)`. Super Pro is approved by a person, not automatically. Check their record (jobs, rating, disputes, complaints) before approving.

### VIP (customers, super admin)

VIP is Trothen's highest membership for customers: **80% off Trothen's service fee** on their bookings, at no cost. It **can't be bought**: only a super admin can grant it, with `Grant VIP`, and remove it with `Revoke VIP`. The customer is told either way.

## Accounts closed by their owners

Anyone can close their own account. Closed accounts can't sign in and are hidden. Here you decide what happens next:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Reopen` | The person can sign in again (for example, they changed their mind). |
| `Erase details` | **Permanently** removes their name, email, phone, address, photos and ID files. The account can never be reopened. Their bookings, payments, reviews and disputes stay on record as "Closed account". |

Before erasing, check: no open dispute, no payout waiting, no money owed. Erasing can't be undone.

> **Policy:** How long Trothen keeps a closed account's details before erasing them is a legal decision for Trothen (data protection rules). Until it is set, erase only when the person asks, or with a super admin's agreement.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Revealing contact details out of curiosity.
- Resetting a password without confirming who is asking.
- Leaving a hold in place after deciding.
- Suspending someone with bookings in progress without telling a super admin.
- Erasing an account that still has a dispute or a payout.
