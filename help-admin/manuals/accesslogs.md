## What this part of Admin is for

The **Access Logs** are a permanent record of **which admin viewed sensitive information, and when**. Knowing who *could* see something is not enough; data protection rules expect Trothen to know who *did*.

Super admins and the Legal team can open them.

## What is logged

Every time an admin:

- reveals a person's **contact details** (Customers & Providers);
- opens the full **list of people**;
- opens an **ID document** or face photo;
- opens **disputes** or their evidence;
- views **financial** data: transactions, the funds by region, token balances, payment codes;
- presses **Show full number** on a payout, marks a payout **sent**, sends by MTN, or reports a problem;
- confirms or rejects a **token** or **customer payment**, records a payment or adjusts a balance;
- opens an **Admin manual**.

## The page

**Recent Access** shows each event: the **admin** (name and email), **what they viewed**, their **IP address**, and **when**.

## Using the logs

- **Regular check:** once a week, look for anything unusual: someone viewing many contact details, or ID files outside their work, or at strange hours.
- **After a complaint:** if someone says their details were misused, find who viewed them, and when.
- **When someone leaves:** check what they viewed in their last weeks.

> **Rule:** The logs are evidence. They can't be changed or deleted from Admin, and they are kept even when other data is cleaned up.

> **Policy:** How long access logs are kept, and who reviews them how often, is for Trothen to set with its lawyer. Until set, review them weekly and keep them for at least one year.

## If you find misuse

1. Don't confront the person first.
2. Note the events (screenshot or write them down, inside Trothen's systems).
3. Tell a super admin or your manager. They may suspend the account while it is looked into.
