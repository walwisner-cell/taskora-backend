## What this part of Admin is for

**Locations & Admins** is where a **super admin** creates and manages the accounts of the Trothen team: a **regional admin** for a city or a country, and **team members** with one job (verification, disputes, finance, customer service, legal, sales, HR). A **team manager** can also add people to their own team, in **My team**.

> **Rule:** Give each person **only the access their job needs**, and only for **their area**. An admin account can see personal data and move money. When someone leaves, **suspend their account the same day**.

## The roles

| Role | What they can do | Area |
|---|---|---|
| **Regional Admin** | Full access for their area: approvals, people, verification, disputes, payments and more. | One city, or the whole country |
| **Verification Team** | Identity verification: the queue, guarantors, approvals, stores and goods. | All regions, unless limited |
| **Disputes Team** | Disputes, and Fraud & Safety. | All regions, unless limited |
| **Financial Team** | Escrow, payouts, tokens and customer payments; sends payouts. | All regions, unless limited |
| **Accountant**, **Controller** | The same access as Financial, but they **don't send payouts**. | All regions, unless limited |
| **Customer Service** | Approvals, verification and disputes; **no** contact details of people. | All regions, unless limited |
| **Legal** | **Read-only**: disputes, transactions and access logs. | All regions, unless limited |
| **Sales Team** | Custom plan leads and organization accounts only. | Everywhere |
| **HR Team** | Job applications only. | All regions, unless limited |
| **Custom permissions** | Exactly the parts and actions you tick (below). | The area you choose |

**Super admins** can do everything, including creating admins, Go-Live, Data Cleanup and platform settings. A custom admin is **never** a super admin.

## Adding an admin

1. Press `+ Add Admin`.
2. **Admin Name** and **Admin Email** (their work email).
3. **Temporary Password:** a new random password is made for each admin. **Give it to them directly**, by phone or in person, never in a message others can read. They **must choose their own** the first time they sign in.
4. **Country** and **City**.
5. **This admin covers:** **the whole country** (recommended: people type city names in many ways, so a city-only admin can miss people) or **only the one city**.
6. **Role:** choose one from the list above.
7. For a **team** role:
   - **Make this person the team's manager**: they can add people to their own team, and suspend them;
   - **Limit this role to the area chosen** above, instead of everywhere.
8. Save.

### Custom permissions

For **Custom permissions**, each part of Admin is set to **Off**, **View** (see only), **Manage** (see and act), or **Custom** (tick exactly which actions). `Details` lists the actions of each part, for example in Customers & Providers:

- See the list of people;
- See phone numbers and email addresses;
- Pause, suspend or reactivate accounts;
- Put payouts on hold or release them;
- Propose a pro's commission rate.

**Start from a role…** fills the boxes like a standard role, so you only change what differs. "See" is always included when anything else in a part is ticked: nobody can act on what they can't see. Anything not ticked is refused.

> **Tip:** Start from the closest role, then switch **off** what this person doesn't need. It is safer than starting from everything.

## Cities Under Your Oversight

Each regional admin's city: the country, the admin, what they **cover**, the number of users, and the status.

- `Make country-wide`: extend a city admin to their whole country.
- `Suspend`: stop the account at once (for example when someone leaves). `Reactivate` restores it.
- `Delete`: remove the location. Use Suspend instead when you may need the history.

## Functional Team Members

Each team member: their role, area, whether they are a team manager, and whether they are active. `Suspend` or `Reactivate` each one.

## My team (team managers)

A **team manager** sees **My team** in their menu. There they can **add people to their own team** (with the same role, never a higher one) and **suspend** them. They can't change anyone's role or give access beyond their team.

## When someone joins, changes job or leaves

- **Joins:** create their account with the smallest role that fits. Tell them to read the Manuals for their parts of Admin.
- **Changes job:** change their role (or create a new account and suspend the old one), the same day.
- **Leaves:** **suspend the account the same day**, before they leave the building if possible.

> **Policy:** Who may approve a new admin account, and how often access is reviewed, is for Trothen to set. Until set, review every admin account **every 3 months**, and suspend any not used in 30 days.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Sharing one admin account between people.
- Giving the Regional Admin role when a team role would do.
- Sending a temporary password by email or chat.
- Leaving a former employee's account active.
