Why Trothen Exists
Local services shouldn't require a leap of faith.
From Atlanta to Lagos to Accra, hiring a plumber or a tutor has always meant crossing your fingers — no way to know who's really showing up, and no recourse if it goes wrong. Trothen closes that gap: every professional is identity-verified before they can accept a single job, every booking runs on an auto-generated contract, and your payment sits safely in escrow until the work is done right. Trust shouldn't be a gamble. On Trothen, it's the default.
