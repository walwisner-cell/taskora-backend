    Hiring someone local shouldn't be a gamble.

    Most people find a plumber or a tutor through a friend of a friend and hope it works out. If it doesn't, there's usually nobody to call. Trothen checks every pro's government ID before they can take a job, puts the price and the work in writing for every booking, and holds the payment until you say the job is done. If something goes wrong, our dispute team steps in.
